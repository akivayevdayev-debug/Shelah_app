🔒 Your Privacy Matters: Sh'elah does not sell your personal data. We collect only what we need to provide the Service and improve it. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address via our authentication provider (Clerk). We do not store your password directly — it is managed securely by Clerk.

Age Requirement

Every page displays a footer notice that using the Service requires you to be at least 13 years old (16+ if located in the EU/UK), per our Terms of Service. We do not verify age or collect your date of birth — continued use of the Service is treated as your confirmation that you meet this requirement.

Usage Data

We collect information about how you use the Service:

  • Questions asked and AI responses received
  • Pages visited and features used
  • Timestamps of activity
  • Bookmarks, notes, and saved preferences

Preferences

We store your user preferences to personalize your experience, including: language (English/Hebrew), theme (light/dark), community/tradition settings, and calendar display preferences.

Device & Technical Information

We automatically collect certain technical information when you use the Service:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • IP address (for rate-limiting, security, and fraud prevention)
  • Error logs and AI usage/cost logs (request metadata, not full conversation content, beyond what §1 already discloses)
  • Referring URLs

Cookies & Local Storage

We use cookies and similar technologies:

  • Necessary cookies: Authentication session (Clerk), location preferences — required for core functionality
  • Browser localStorage: theme and language preference — stored only on your device and never transmitted to our servers
  • Analytics: Vercel Speed Insights / Web Analytics — cookieless and privacy-first: aggregated, non-personally-identifiable page and performance metrics, no cross-site tracking

Today the Service uses no non-essential, consent-gated cookies. If we add any in the future, we will gate them behind a consent prompt for EU/UK/California users before they load. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings; necessary cookies cannot be disabled without breaking core sign-in functionality.

4. Data Sharing & Sub-Processors

We do not sell or share (as defined by CCPA/CPRA) your personal data to third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We share data with the following service providers ('sub-processors') who help us operate the Service, each under its own data-processing terms:

  • Clerk (clerk.com, US) — Authentication. Stores your name, email, and handles login security.
  • Supabase (supabase.com) — Database hosting. Stores your preferences, bookmarks, and query history.
  • Vercel (vercel.com, US) — Application hosting, request logs, and cookieless aggregated analytics.
  • Google (Google Gemini API, US) — Your question text is sent to Google's AI model to generate answers. No account identifiers (name/email) are sent, only the question text and retrieved source excerpts.
  • Anthropic (Claude API, US) — Fallback/primary AI model depending on routing. Same conditions as Google above.
  • Sefaria (sefaria.org) — Source-text retrieval. Receives search/lookup queries derived from your question, not your identity.
  • Hebcal (hebcal.com) — Jewish calendar, holiday, and zmanim (halachic times) data, looked up by date and your general location (city/coordinates), not your identity.
  • MyMemory / Google Translate — Used as a fallback translation service for source-text passages when needed. Receives text snippets, not your identity.
  • Sentry (sentry.io) — Error tracking. Receives crash/error reports with question and answer text scrubbed before transmission (see docs/OBSERVABILITY.md).

We may share anonymized, aggregated data (no personal information) for academic or halachic research purposes.

We will disclose your data if required by law (e.g., subpoena, court order), or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the safety of any person or to prevent illegal activity.

6. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA & Others)

Depending on your location, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you — signed-in users can self-serve this instantly via "Privacy & Data" in the account menu
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your account and all associated personal data — signed-in users can self-serve this instantly via "Privacy & Data" in the account menu, which also deletes your sign-in identity
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format (JSON) — available instantly via the same self-serve download tool
  • Opt-out / Do Not Sell or Share: We do not sell or share personal data as those terms are defined by CCPA/CPRA, so there is nothing to opt out of today; we honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal as a valid opt-out request for any future data sale/sharing regardless. You can also disable non-essential analytics cookies and unsubscribe from service emails.
  • Objection & Restriction: Object to, or request we restrict, processing of your data for certain purposes
  • Withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent (e.g., age attestation, any future non-essential cookies), withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal
  • Non-discrimination: We will not deny you the Service, charge you a different price, or provide a different level of service because you exercised any of these rights

Access and deletion are available instantly, with no need to email us, from "Privacy & Data" in the account menu once you're signed in. For correction, objection/restriction, or any request you can't complete there, email akiva.yevda@gmail.com with your account email and the specific request. We will respond within 30 days (or the timeframe required by applicable law, if shorter).

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS 1.2+)
  • Passwords are hashed and salted by Clerk — we never see your password
  • Database data is encrypted at rest (Supabase default encryption), and Row-Level Security policies restrict each user's data to that user
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel
  • We conduct regular security reviews

Breach notification: if a security incident results in unauthorized access to your personal data, we will notify affected users and, where legally required, the relevant supervisory authority, without undue delay and in any case within the timeframe required by applicable law (e.g., 72 hours under GDPR Art. 33 once we become aware of a reportable breach), describing what happened, the data involved, and the steps we are taking.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to, and may not be used by, anyone under 13 years old (or under 16 in the EU/UK, absent verifiable parental consent — see our Terms of Service §3). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone below these thresholds. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a user below the applicable threshold, we will delete that account and its data. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information in violation of this policy, please contact us so we can investigate and delete it.
